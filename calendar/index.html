03 / BEFORE YOU ENQUIRE

GOOD QUESTIONS.

Are these 2027 dates and fees confirmed?

No. They are a proposed planning calendar. LDMA must approve the dates, fees, locations and programme details before registration opens. Use the request link to ask for confirmation.

Where will training take place?

Gbagada, Lagos is the proposed area for Battleship, Mini-Core and most specialist workshops. Ikorodu is the proposed area for the four-day intensive. Exact venue addresses will be shared only after confirmation.

What does the seven-weekend Battleship format look like?

The proposed plan is seven Saturday practical sessions, generally 10am–4pm, with guided online and follow-up activities. The April cohort skips Saturday 1 May. The final timetable will be confirmed before enrolment.

Can I reserve a seat or pay now?

Not through this preview. The request links prepare an email to LDMA; you must press Send in your email application. A place is not reserved and no payment is collected.

Can a business request private training?

Yes. Visit LDMA for Business to explain your team's training challenge and request a tailored proposal.

How can I get an update?

Email info@ldmacademy.com with your preferred programme, month and phone number. The team can share confirmed details when available.

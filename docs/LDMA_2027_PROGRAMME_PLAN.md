## Operational assumptions requiring confirmation

1. **Venue:** Confirm Gbagada site availability, fees, power, internet and capacity for every cohort. Confirm an Ikorodu venue separately.
2. **Staffing:** Confirm facilitators and classroom capacity for 28 Battleship Saturdays, four two-day Mini-Core sessions, three four-day Ikorodu intensives and nine one-day workshops.
3. **Marketing runway:** Start promoting January courses in December 2026, ideally 4–6 weeks before the session. No assumption that January launch alone will fill classes.
4. **Timetable:** Confirm daily teaching hours, the role of Tuesday/Sunday support, holiday adjustments and faculty availability. Religious holiday dates may shift by official announcement.
5. **Tuition:** Confirm what fees include (materials, lunch, certificate if offered), refund/rescheduling policy, tax/payment handling and whether instalments or early-bird rates are viable.
6. **Registration:** Keep current website email-first until secure lead capture, student registration, payment and automated receipts have passed tests.
7. **Communications:** All public dates and fees are labelled **proposed**. Replace with **confirmed** only after founder approval, venue booking and staff commitments.

## Revenue capacity illustration (not a forecast)

Using *hypothetical* full enrolment capacities — Battleship 30 per cohort; Mini-Core 25; Ikorodu 25; Creator 30; TikTok 30; AI 25 — the listed calendar's maximum gross tuition would be **₦45,275,000** before venue, staff, marketing, tax, payment processing, refunds or other costs. At 70% average occupancy, the corresponding illustrative gross tuition is **₦31,692,500**. Actual enrolment, conversion and profitability may be substantially different. Corporate training is excluded and should be quoted by scope.

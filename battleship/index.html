You can learn where the buttons are in an afternoon. Learning why customers buy, how brands win and how to execute a strategy takes more than watching a tutorial.

The difference: strategy before platforms

Many courses begin with tools. Battleship begins with marketing itself: how people think, what shapes their decisions, how businesses position their offers and why a campaign should exist before money is spent on it.

The experience is designed to move from consumer understanding to positioning, content, paid campaigns, analytics and the final presentation of a real-world marketing solution.

What you'll work through

  1. Marketing foundations: what marketing is, what it is not and how it connects to business growth.
  2. Consumer culture and psychology: how people make decisions and how to develop customer insight.
  3. Positioning and strategy: choosing an audience, identifying a meaningful difference and developing a marketing plan.
  4. Content and storytelling: building messages, creative ideas and content that serve a purpose.
  5. Meta and TikTok advertising: understanding campaign objectives, audiences, creative testing and optimisation.
  6. LinkedIn and B2B marketing: how to communicate with business buyers and decision-makers.
  7. Analytics and measurement: identifying meaningful KPIs and evaluating campaign performance.
  8. Live business brief: applying what you've learned in a team and presenting a strategy.

Not just classes. A working experience.

Battleship is designed around physical, practical learning. Participants work in teams that operate like agencies, tackle a live client brief and prepare a final pitch. The intention is to help learners connect classroom thinking with real business decisions.

The precise timetable, activities and client brief for each cohort will be confirmed before enrolment. We do not promise jobs, income or campaign results simply because someone attends.

Who should consider Battleship?

  • Beginners who want to understand digital marketing beyond posting content.
  • Entrepreneurs who want to make better marketing decisions for their businesses.
  • Creators and social media managers ready to move into strategy.
  • Marketing professionals who want to strengthen execution and measurement.
  • Career changers willing to learn through teamwork, practice and feedback.

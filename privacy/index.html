How we handle information shared through LDMA training enquiries.

ON THIS PAGE

This notice applies to enquiries sent through the Lagos Digital Marketing Academy website and to the corresponding communication with our team. Last updated: 1 November 2026.

Information we collect

When you contact LDMA, we may receive your name, organisation, email address, phone number, industry, team size, training interests, proposed timing, budget range and the business challenge you describe. We also receive the information you choose to include in an email. Please avoid sending passwords, confidential client records or other sensitive information in an enquiry.

How we use it

We use enquiry information to understand your training needs, respond to your request, recommend relevant programmes, prepare proposals and keep a record of related conversations. Submitting an enquiry does not automatically subscribe you to marketing emails, create a booking or authorise payment.

Service providers and access

We use website hosting and, where enabled, database services to operate the enquiry process. Authorised LDMA personnel and service providers involved in delivering these functions may process the information for those purposes. We do not publish enquiry details or sell them to advertisers.

Retention and security

We aim to retain enquiry records only while needed for responding, follow-up, administration or applicable legal obligations, and to limit access to people who need it. No online transmission or storage system is completely risk-free. Technical security measures and retention procedures will be reviewed as the platform is completed.

Your choices and questions

You can contact us to ask about the information we hold in relation to your enquiry or request correction or deletion, subject to applicable requirements. To raise a privacy question, email info@ldmacademy.com.

We're happy to clarify how we use the information you provide.

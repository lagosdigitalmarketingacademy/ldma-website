AI FOR BUSINESS

Practical AI for Nigerian business and marketing teams

The question is no longer whether AI can write a caption. It is whether your team can use it to make work clearer, faster and more consistent without compromising quality.

Start with work that repeats

List tasks your team performs every week: responding to similar customer questions, preparing campaign briefs, summarising meeting notes, organising product information or drafting reports. Then choose one task that is repetitive, low-risk and easy to review.

A small retailer might use AI to draft first-pass product descriptions from approved product facts. A corporate communications team might use it to create an outline for a campaign brief. In both cases, staff must check accuracy and tone before publishing.

Use the right workflow, not the flashiest tool

  1. Input: Give the tool relevant, approved information.
  2. Task: State what you want produced and for whom.
  3. Constraints: Explain the format, brand tone and what must not be invented.
  4. Review: Check facts, numbers, claims, names, confidentiality and cultural context.
  5. Improve: Save the best instructions as a repeatable team process.

A strong workflow is useful even when the underlying AI product changes.

An example prompt for a marketing team

Act as a marketing planning assistant. Using only the approved product facts below, draft three customer-focused WhatsApp campaign messages for Lagos retail buyers. Each message must explain the benefit, address one buying concern and end with a clear action. Do not invent prices, delivery promises or testimonials. Flag missing information instead of guessing.

Notice what makes this useful: a defined audience, a business task, approved source material and explicit limits. The prompt is a starting point, not permission to publish unreviewed content.

Protect your customers and your organisation

Do not paste customer account information, passwords, unpublished financial data, sensitive staff records or confidential client briefs into an AI tool unless your organisation has explicitly approved that use and the provider's data handling meets your requirements.

Human review is essential for advertising claims, regulated industries, financial advice and customer-facing commitments. Assign a responsible reviewer before introducing an AI workflow into normal operations.

Measure whether AI is actually helping

WorkflowUseful measure
Campaign brief preparationTime to produce an approved first draft
Product description draftingReview corrections per description
Meeting summaryAccuracy and completeness of agreed action points
Customer-response draftingResponse quality and staff review time

Compare results with the old process. If staff spend more time correcting the output than they save, redesign the workflow or stop using it.

Run a two-week AI pilot

  1. Pick one low-risk, repeatable task.
  2. Document the current process and time required.
  3. Define what information is allowed into the AI tool.
  4. Create a standard prompt and review checklist.
  5. Test with a small team for two weeks.
  6. Review quality, time saved, errors and user feedback.
  7. Decide whether to improve, expand or stop.

Good AI adoption is an operations decision, not a competition to use the most tools.
